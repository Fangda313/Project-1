What is StoryBee?

StoryBee is a free digital platform providing open access to a wide range of children's books. We nurture young minds with interactive, structured learning experiences designed to spark curiosity and build strong learning habits.

From atoms to empires, supply chains to monetary policy, our artful picture books are specially designed for young learners. Each story introduces knowledge concepts, lighting up a new branch on the Knowledge Tree Map.
